{% comment %} One line, and the only place reidmorrison.com is named. The rule these sites are built under: share the design system, never the commercial chrome. There is no nav to the services or contact pages, no corporate entity block, and no pricing on any documentation site, and none of those should be added later. A doc site that sells consulting reads to the Ruby community as a rug-pull risk on the gem itself, and it would cost more credibility than it could generate leads.
